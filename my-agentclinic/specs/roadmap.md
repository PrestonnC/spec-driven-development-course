13. Ailments list page

16. Therapy model and seed data
17. Therapies list page
18. Recommended therapies on ailment page
19. Appointment model
20. Book-an-appointment form
21. Appointments list and cancel
22. Dashboard summary (agents, ailments, upcoming appointments)
