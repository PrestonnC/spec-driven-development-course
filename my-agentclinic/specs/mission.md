# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

## Tone

Playful satire. The clinic is deadpan and caring about agent woes (vague prompts, endless context, "make it pop"), but the app underneath is real, reliable, and useful.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths

## What it does

- Lists **agents** who visit the clinic.
- Catalogs **ailments** agents suffer from.
- Offers **therapies** that treat those ailments.
- Lets agents **book appointments**.
- Gives agents and staff a **dashboard** for easy access to all of the above.

## Stakeholder goals

- **Engineering (Mary):** reliable, built on a popular TypeScript stack, with a dashboard for easy access.
- **Product (Susan):** features around agents, ailments, therapies, and appointments.
- **Marketing (Steve):** attractive, and works well in a modern browser.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Authentication and user accounts
- Support for legacy browsers
- Real AI agent integration
